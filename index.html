Our Story

Scoop & Swirl began with a hand-crank churn and a summer that was far too hot. Today we serve from a restored shipping container on the boardwalk, steps from the waves, using milk from a nearby dairy and fruit picked that week.

Every batch is small, slow and made by hand. Best enjoyed with sand between your toes.
